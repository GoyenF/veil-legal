Veil Support

Blur faces, license plates and private details in photos and videos, on-device.

Getting help

Questions, bug reports or feature requests: open an issue on GitHub. Please include your iPhone or iPad model and iOS version.

Subscriptions

Veil Pro is billed by Apple. To cancel or change your plan, open Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions on your device. To restore a purchase on a new device, open Veil › Settings › Restore Purchases.

Privacy

Veil processes everything on your device and collects no data. Read the privacy policy.
