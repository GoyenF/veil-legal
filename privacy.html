Veil Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Veil is built so your photos and videos never leave your device.

What Veil processes Photos and videos you choose are analyzed on your device with Apple's Vision framework to find faces, license plates and text. Rendering and exporting also happen on your device.

What Veil collects Nothing. Veil has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it doesn't send your media or any information about you to the developer or to third parties.

Photo library Veil reads only the items you pick in the system photo picker. With your permission, it saves redacted results to your library.

Metadata By default, Veil removes location, device and capture metadata from exported files. You can turn this off in Settings.

Instagram When you share to Instagram, Veil hands the redacted file to the Instagram app on your device. From then on, Instagram's privacy policy applies.

Purchases Subscriptions and purchases are handled by Apple. Veil only receives confirmation of which features you've unlocked.

Your settings Settings such as the metadata toggle are stored on your device only.

Changes If this policy changes, the updated version will appear here and in the app.
